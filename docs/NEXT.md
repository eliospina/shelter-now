# Next version: ideas saved for later

Ideas discussed and agreed to do after the current release.

## Reaching the bunker without internet

- **Landmarks on "Your path".** Draw lakes, rivers, motorways, railways, large
  parks and known places (zoo, hospital, station) under the walked path, and
  say them in text ("On the way: Brunnsviken (lake), E4"). Data: Lantmäteriet
  open data or OpenStreetMap, simplified to a few MB for all of Sweden and
  bundled with the app. Estimated about a day of work. Most useful from a few
  hundred metres up to about 2 km.
- **Position radar.** You in the centre, the nearest bunker and the two others
  drawn in their direction and at their distance, turning with the phone.
- **Street map with a route inside the app.** Offline map (OpenStreetMap)
  downloaded in advance for the user's area. The most complete option, but it
  makes the app much larger: a separate project.
- **Plain sentence under the compass.** "The arrow shows the straight-line
  direction. Follow the streets towards it; the distance drops as you get
  closer."
- **"Show someone".** A full-screen card with the address in Swedish ("Var
  ligger skyddsrummet på …?") to show a neighbour or a police officer.
